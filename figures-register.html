Tasmanian justice figures

Sources and checks.

Every figure on our adult and youth justice figures pages, with where it comes from, who it counts, the period it covers, whether the source says it can be compared between states, and when we last checked it against the original.

What the labels mean

Official figureA number or fact published by the source — the Productivity Commission, the ABS or the AIHW — reproduced as published, or worked out directly from published numbers.

Official noteThe source's own definition, footnote or warning about a figure.

InterpretationOur reading of the figures. It is supported by the numbers, the counting method or the source's footnotes, but the source itself does not say it.

Our questionA judgement or question the Foundation is raising. It is not a finding, and the figures alone do not settle it.

How these were checked. Figures were transcribed from the published data tables of the 2026 Report on Government Services, AIHW Youth Justice in Australia 2024–25 and ABS Prisoners in Australia 2025 and Recorded Crime – Offenders 2024–25, then checked again against those tables and the reports' own text in November 2026.

Where a record says "not yet recorded", that figure's details have not yet been confirmed against the source. Corrections are welcome: contact us.
