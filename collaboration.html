03

Just Desserts

Active

A community support group for the Drug Court's Mandated Diversion (CMD) program, founded by Board member Jess Long (formerly known as Jess Smith) and dedicated to promoting therapeutic jurisprudence and alternatives to imprisonment. The committee meets monthly to discuss the program, upcoming graduations, and emerging research, and funds tangible incentives for participants responding well to the program.
