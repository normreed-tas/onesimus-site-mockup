What "Onesimus" means

A Greek name meaning "useful, or profitable."

From the biblical book of Philemon: a man once written off as useless finds, through a chance meeting in prison, a way back to being valued by the people around him. Our mission is to help people leaving prison find that same welcome — to work with people with convictions, their families, and communities, so that on release they come home as useful, profitable, and fulfilled members of society.
