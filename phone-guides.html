Onesimus builds and hosts them. The content belongs to whoever wrote it.

For other organisations

Have something that should be on a phone?

If your organisation has a booklet, handbook or service list that people struggle to carry around, we can put it on a phone. There is no charge, and we are not trying to take it off you.

What we bring

  • The app itself — already built, already working
  • A plain-language rewrite, with your original wording kept one tap away
  • Offline access and add-to-home-screen, so it works with no signal
  • Hosting, at no cost
  • A reviewer view, so your people can check every detail before anyone sees it
  • One list of services, so a changed phone number is fixed once and updates everywhere

What you bring

  • The content, and the authority to publish it
  • Someone who can confirm the details are right
  • Your name on it
  • A willingness to tell us when something changes

What we are not doing

  • The content stays yours. We do not claim it and we do not rebrand it.
  • Nothing is collected from the people who use it. No accounts, no tracking, no analytics. Anything a person ticks or writes stays on their own phone.
  • You can take it elsewhere whenever you like. The files are plain text and we will hand them over.
  • We will not publish anything about your service that you have not confirmed.

This grew out of two pieces of work rather than a plan. We built Getting Out to show the Tasmanian Health Service what a booklet could be, then built Stay Connected for families because nobody else was going to. Having built the thing twice, it costs us very little to build it again for somebody else.
