You are not the only one

Roughly one in three people entering prison has children depending on them. Nobody counts those children.

Australian prisons do not routinely record whether the people they receive have children, so there is no official figure — nationally or for Tasmania. That absence is part of why families are left to work this out alone, and it is one of the things Onesimus is trying to change.

What we are working towards

Where this section is going

Onesimus is not currently a principal service provider in this space. What we hold is thirty years of practice knowledge, a published guide and the relationships to keep it accurate. This is how we intend to put it to work.

Now

Get it accurate

  • Confirm current Tasmanian practice with Tasmania Prison Service
  • Build the source register — what exists, where, and whether it is still current
  • Rebuild the child's diary as a web edition
Next

Publish the family pages

  • The three stages above, in plain language, phone-first
  • The children's material, usable on a screen rather than reprinted
  • A clear route to the people who can actually help, inside and outside the prison
Then

Keep it alive

  • An annual check against Tasmania Prison Service practice, as we already do for the justice figures
  • Material offered to any Tasmanian service that wants it, under our name or theirs
  • The generational thread carried into youth justice work
