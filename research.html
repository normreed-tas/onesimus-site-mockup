Research

The evidence and ideas shaping what's possible.

From local inspection reports to international models of reform, this is the research that informs Onesimus's advocacy — and the case for what Tasmania's justice system could become.

An international model

Singapore fell from 18,000 prisoners to 8,000. Could Tasmania learn from how?

Conceived by former Deputy Director Jason Wong, Singapore's Yellow Ribbon Project reshaped a prison system around Chua Chin Kiat's "Captains of Lives" vision — treating rehabilitation, not punishment, as the organising purpose of custody. Tasmania's size and community feel may make it uniquely placed to learn from this model. Jason is visiting Tasmania in December 2026 to meet with the Minister for Corrections.
